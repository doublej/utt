Hold a key, speak, release. The text lands at your cursor. Nothing leaves the
machine.

This one can fix your grammar before the text is pasted, and the live words
now write themselves onto the card.

## Correct grammar

A new switch under Text → Cleanup runs the macOS grammar checker over the
transcript and applies its first suggestion for each issue. It works with or
without model cleanup turned on.

It checks grammar only, not spelling, so names stay as you said them.
Dictation that mixes languages can confuse it: when it wants to change more
than a quarter of the words, your text is pasted untouched. It is off by
default.

## Live words write themselves in

While you hold the key, each new letter fades up and settles into place, and
letters already on the card stay still. When the transcript is ready, the live
words move into the finished text instead of blinking out. With Reduce Motion
turned on, the text just appears.
